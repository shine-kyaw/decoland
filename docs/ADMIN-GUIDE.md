# Deco-Land website: staff guide

Sign in at **decolandgroup.com/admin** with your email and password. If you forget the password, use "Forgot password?" on the sign-in page.

The admin works like WordPress. The menu on the left is grouped as follows:

| Group | What's inside |
|---|---|
| **Dashboard** | Today's visits, new enquiries, job applications and recent changes |
| **Content** | Pages, News & CSR posts, Post categories |
| **Catalogue** | Products, Product categories, Brands |
| **Company** | Group companies, Jobs, Downloads |
| **Inbox** | Enquiries, Job applications |
| **Media** | Every photo and PDF |
| **Settings** | Site settings (phone, email, addresses, hours, alert emails) and Menus |
| **Users** | Who can sign in. Administrators only |

There are three roles:
- **Administrator** can do everything.
- **Editor** works on content, the catalogue and media.
- **Sales** sees only the Inbox.

## English and Burmese

Every text field has an English version and a Burmese version.

1. Open the item and write the English text.
2. Switch **Locale** (top right) from *English* to *မြန်မာ*.
3. Write the Burmese text in the same fields, then save.

Photos, numbers and links are shared by both languages, so you only add them once. If a Burmese field is left empty, the website shows the English text there instead.

## Saving, drafts and publishing

The panel at the top of each page works like WordPress's Publish box:

- **Save draft** keeps your changes without showing them on the website.
- **Publish changes** puts them live within a few seconds.
- **Schedule** (the arrow next to Publish) publishes at a date and time you choose, for example a news post for Monday morning.
- **Preview** (the square-arrow icon) opens the page as it will look.
- **Versions** lists every earlier save. Open one and press *Restore* to go back to it.

Drafts are saved automatically every few seconds while you type.

## Editing a page

Go to **Content → Pages** and open a page (*Home*, *About*, *Paper* and so on).

Each page is a stack of **sections**:
- Click a section to open it and edit its text and photos.
- **Drag** the ⋮⋮ handle to change the order.
- Use **⋯ → Remove** to delete a section, or **⋯ → Duplicate** to copy it.
- **Add section** at the bottom adds a new one. Every section is pre-designed, so the page always looks right.

| Section | Use it for |
|---|---|
| Hero slider | Big changing photos at the top of the home page |
| Page header | The photo and title at the top of other pages |
| Headline and figures | A statement with numbers (1997, 25,000 m², …) |
| Image cards | Two to six cards with a photo, text and a link |
| Product categories / Category list with products | Pulled automatically from the catalogue |
| Product showcase | Featured products in a sliding row |
| Brand logos | Pulled automatically from Catalogue → Brands |
| Facility (dark band) | A large photo plus two smaller ones, with figures |
| Services | Cards with photos and bullet lists |
| Numbered steps | A process, such as printing steps |
| Timeline | Years and milestones |
| Text and image | Any text beside a photo |
| Photo gallery | A grid of photos |
| Group companies / Group company cards | Pulled from Company → Group companies |
| Latest news, Open jobs, Downloads | Pulled from those sections automatically |
| Contact band, Contact options and form, Addresses and map | Contact details come from Site settings |
| Call to action | A coloured band with one button |

**New page:** Content → Pages → *Create new*. Give it a title and add sections. It appears at `decolandgroup.com/en/<address>`. To show it in the menu, add it in **Settings → Menus**.

## Photos

- Every photo slot has an upload box. You can drag a file in, or choose one already in the **Media library**.
- Use clear, real photos: landscape for headers and banners, square or 4:3 for products.
- Large photos are fine. The website shrinks them automatically for phones.
- Fill in the **Alt text**: a short description such as "Paper rolls in the Hlaing Thar Yar warehouse". It helps search engines and blind visitors.

## Products

Go to **Catalogue → Products**.

- **Title, summary, photos, specifications.** Specifications are label and value pairs, for example *GSM / 70*, *Size / A4*, *Pages / 80*, *Pack / 12 pcs*.
- **Category** (right side) decides where the product appears: Paper, APOLO stationery or Services.
- **Featured** puts the product in the showcase on the home page and the Stationery page.
- **Order:** lower numbers show first.
- **There are no prices.** Every product page has an "Enquire about this product" button, and the enquiry arrives in the Inbox already naming the product.

## News & CSR

Go to **Content → News & CSR → Create new**. Add a title, category (News, CSR or Events), date, cover photo, short excerpt, the story and an optional photo gallery. Publish, or schedule it.

## Jobs

Go to **Company → Jobs**. Add the title, department, location, type, closing date and description. Untick **Accepting applications** to close a job without deleting it. Applications, including CVs, arrive in **Inbox → Job applications**. Only signed-in staff can open the CVs.

## Downloads

Go to **Company → Downloads**. Upload a PDF (company profile, catalogue or certificate), give it a title and choose the category. It appears on the Downloads page.

## The inbox

Every enquiry and job application from the website is saved under **Inbox**, and also sent by email. A message to the sales DingTalk and Telegram groups is sent too, once those have been connected.

For each enquiry:
1. Open it. You can see who sent it, their phone (and whether they're OK with Viber), the product and the message.
2. Set **Status** to *New*, *Contacted*, *Quoted* or *Closed*.
3. **Assign** it to the person handling it.
4. Add **Internal notes**, such as "Called, sending quote Friday". Customers never see these.

Use **Filters** on the list to see, for example, only *New* enquiries. The Dashboard shows counts by status.

## Site settings and menus

- **Settings → Site settings** holds the phone, Viber, email, Messenger link, both addresses (English and Burmese), **opening hours** and the alert email list. A change here updates every page.
- **Settings → Menus** holds the header and footer links.

## If something goes wrong

- **A change isn't showing:** check it was *published*, not only saved as a draft. Then refresh the website.
- **A page looks wrong after an edit:** open **Versions** and restore the last good one.
- **Anything else:** contact Aster, and include a screenshot and the page address.
